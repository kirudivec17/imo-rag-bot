FSS CODE - International Code for Fire Safety Systems - Detailed Summary based on MSC.98(73) and amendments
Adopted by MSC 73, mandatory under SOLAS Chapter II-2 Regulation 3.

Chapter 1 - International Shore Connection: Standard dimensions, flange 178mm outer, 64mm inner, 4 bolts 16mm, suitable for 1 MPa. Required on all ships.

Chapter 2 - Personal Protection: Firefighter outfits: breathing apparatus, protective clothing, boots, gloves, helmet, safety lamp, axe. Minimum 2 outfits cargo ships, 4 passenger ships. EEBD requirements.

Chapter 3 - Fixed Gas Extinguishing: CO2, FM-200, Novec 1230. CO2 quantity 40% gross volume machinery space, 35% cargo pump room. Discharge 85% within 2 mins. Alarms and time delays.

Chapter 4 - Fixed Foam: Foam concentrate, expansion ratio, supply rate 1.0-1.5L/min/m2 for cargo tanks. Monitor capacity, foam station requirements.

Chapter 5 - Fixed Pressure Water Spraying: Nozzles, pump capacity, pressure 0.35MPa, drainage, filters. For machinery spaces and cargo pump rooms.

Chapter 6 - Automatic Sprinkler, Fire Detection and Alarm: Wet, dry, pre-action systems. Sprinkler spacing, coverage 12m2, sensitivity. For accommodation, service spaces.

Chapter 7 - Fixed Fire Detection and Alarm: Smoke, heat, flame detectors. Sensitivity, spacing 9m smoke, 7m heat. Control panel, power supply, testing MSC.1/Circ.1242.

Chapter 8 - Fixed Hydrocarbon Gas Detection: For oil tankers, sampling points, alarm levels 10% LEL pre-alarm, 30% LEL high alarm.

Chapter 9 - Fixed Fire Detection for cargo spaces: Sample extraction smoke detection system. Sampling pipes, fans, sequential scanning.
