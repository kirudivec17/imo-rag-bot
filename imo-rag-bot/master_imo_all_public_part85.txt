SOLAS CHAPTER XI-2:
Reg 1 Definitions: Ship security, port facility, Company Security Officer CSO, Ship Security Officer SSO, Port Facility Security Officer PFSO, Security Level.

Reg 6 Obligations of Company.

Reg 9 Control and compliance measures: Control in port, expulsion.
Reg 10 Regional agreements, Reg 11 Alternative security agreements, Reg 12 Equivalent security arrangements, Reg 13 Communication.

Reg 1 Definitions: Ship security, port facility, Company Security Officer CSO, Ship Security Officer SSO, Port Facility Security Officer PFSO, Security Level.
Reg 2 Application: Passenger ships, cargo >=500GT international, MODUs, port facilities serving such ships.
Reg 3 Obligations of Contracting Governments regarding security.
Reg 4 SOLAS not prejudicial to rights.
Reg 5 Declaration of Security DOS - when required: ship/port interface, ship/ship.
Reg 6 Obligations of Company.
Reg 7 Ship security alert system SSAS - covert alert to Administration.
Reg 8 Master not constrained.
Reg 9 Control and compliance measures: Control in port, expulsion.
Reg 10 Regional agreements, Reg 11 Alternative security agreements, Reg 12 Equivalent security arrangements, Reg 13 Communication.

ISPS PART A:
5 Ship Security: Security Level 1 normal, Level 2 heightened risk, Level 3 exceptional/incident imminent.
6 Company responsibility: ensure Master has info, SSO, CSO accessible.
7 Ship Security: Company must ensure Ship Security Assessment SSA done.
8 SSA: On-scene survey, identification of restricted areas, threats, vulnerabilities. Documented.
9 Ship Security Plan SSP: Approved by Administration, confidential, includes: measures for Level 1-3, restricted areas, cargo handling, delivery stores, baggage, monitoring security, DOS, evacuation, SSO duties, auditing. Must be protected from disclosure. Must have 9 sections minimum.
10 Records: Training, drills, incidents, breaches, changes in security levels, communications - retain 3 years min.
11 CSO: Person ashore ensuring SSA, SSP developed, audits, training, liaison PFSO.
12 SSO: Onboard responsible to Administration and Company, ensure SSP implemented, drills, inspections, security awareness.

14 Port Facility Security: Port Facility Security Assessment PFSA, Port Facility Security Plan PFSP.
15 PFSA: Identification assets, threats, countermeasures.
16 PFSP: Measures for Level 1-3, restricted areas, handling cargo, delivery stores, monitoring.
17 PFSO: Responsible for PFSP development, implementation.
18 Training for port facility.

Security Equipment: Automatic Identification System AIS, LRIT, SSAS testing procedures. Ship Security Alert: 2 activations points on bridge and one in other location.

Drills: Every 3 months crew security drill, every 18 months company exercise with port.

Verification: ISSC International Ship Security Certificate issued after verification, 5 years validity, intermediate verification between 2nd-3rd anniversary. Interim ISSC 6 months.

Guidance Part B paragraphs: B/9.2 SSO knowledge, B/13 training matrix.
Best Management Practices BMP5 for piracy, cyber security MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3 integrated in SSP.
