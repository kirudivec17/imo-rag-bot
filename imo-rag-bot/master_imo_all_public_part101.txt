ELEMENT 6 RESOURCES & PERSONNEL - Manning per Safe Manning, qualified, medical fit, familiarization per STCW A-I/14 - must within 14 days, language working language. Training.

ELEMENT 9 REPORTS & ANALYSIS - Non-conformity NC, accident, hazardous occurrence - reporting to company, investigation, corrective action per MSC-MEPC.7/Circ.8.
