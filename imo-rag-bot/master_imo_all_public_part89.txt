SSP mandatory contents 9: 1) Org, 2) Access control - gangway log, ID cards, 3) Restricted areas - bridge, E/R, steering, 4) Cargo handling - seal check, 5) Stores delivery - search, 6) Monitoring - deck patrols, CCTV, 7) Security alerts, 8) Evacuation, 9) Reporting. Must be locked, access SSO/CSO.

Security Levels detailed: Level 1  - gangway watch, ID check, restricted area locked. Level 2 - additional searches 50% stores, extra patrols, access limited to one point. Level 3 - ship prepared to evacuate, 100% search, port may limit.

SSAS 2 buttons - covert, sends lat/long to Company Security, test monthly per MSC.1/Circ.1470. AIS must be on except Master discretion security.

DRILLS: Security drill every 3 months - bomb search, stowaway search. Exercise every 18 months - tabletop with port facility.

ISSC form - issued by flag or RO, intermediate between 2nd-3rd anniversary +-3 months.

PSC DETENTION GROUNDS: No ISSC, SSP not approved, SSO not trained per A/12, SSAS faulty.
