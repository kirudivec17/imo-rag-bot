CO2 SYSTEM - COMPLETE TECHNICAL DEEP DIVE FOR PRESENTATION

1. PRINCIPLE: CO2 extinguishes by oxygen reduction to <15%, cooling. Concentration 30-50% for surface fire, 35% for machinery space gross volume, 30% for cargo holds net volume. FSS Code Ch 3 para 2.1.1
---

SOURCE: CO2-System-ULTRA-DEEP-FSS-Ch3-2024.txt
URL: IMO FSS Code Chapter 3 CO2 System
2. QUANTITY CALCULATION - MOST IMPORTANT FOR EXAM:
Formula: V_co2 = 0.56 m3 per kg CO2 free gas at STP
Machinery Space: Largest gross volume including casing. Example Calculation:
Engine Room LxBxH 30x15x10 = 4500 m3, plus casing 200 m3 = 4700 m3
For 35% concentration, formula Q = 0.35 x V / 0.56 = 0.625 x V
Actually FSS requires 40% for machinery without casing? Check: For cargo ships machinery 40% of gross excluding shaft but including casing? Simplified: CO2 required = 35% net? Standard used 40% rule: 1kg per 0.56m3 so 40% means 0.40*4700=1880m3 free gas => 1880/0.56=3357kg CO2.
Each cylinder 45kg => 75 cylinders. If 67.5kg cylinder => 50 cylinders.
Cargo hold example: Hold 5000m3 net, 30% => 1500m3 free => 2678kg => 60 cylinders of 45kg.

3. DISCHARGE TIME REQUIREMENTS:
Machinery space: 85% of required quantity within 2 minutes.
Cargo holds: 2/3 within 10 minutes for bulk, but ro-ro cargo 2/3 in 10 min.
High pressure system valve opening - pneumatic time delay 20 sec after alarm.
---

SOURCE: CO2-System-ULTRA-DEEP-FSS-Ch3-2024.txt
URL: IMO FSS Code Chapter 3 CO2 System
4. CYLINDER STORAGE ROOM:
Separate room, temperature <55C, ventilation 6 air changes/hour, door opening outwards. Manifold pressure 58-60 bar at 20C. Weighing or liquid level. Cylinder test 250 bar hydraulic every 10 years, burst disc 190 bar. Dip tube.

5. ALARM AND CONTROLS:
Audible alarm distinct from fire alarm - pneumatic siren 120dB plus visual flashing. Automatic timer 20-30 sec delay, manual release boxes outside protected space with break glass. Two independent controls - one automatic for alarm, second manual for release. Must be lockable - cabinet key in break glass box adjacent. Instructions in multiple languages.

6. PIPING AND NOZZLES:
Pipe thickness schedule 40 for <50mm, schedule 80 >50mm. Nozzles full bore type, uniform distribution, max 5m spacing, 1m from bulkhead. Flow calculation using FSS Ch3 Eq: pressure drop. Low pressure system - insulated tank -20C, 18-22 bar, refrigeration plant 2x. Safety relief valves 2 set at 24 bar, vent to open deck.
---

SOURCE: CO2-System-ULTRA-DEEP-FSS-Ch3-2024.txt
URL: IMO FSS Code Chapter 3 CO2 System
7. LOW vs HIGH PRESSURE:
High: 45-67.5kg cylinders, 55 bar, room required. Low: tank 4-30 tonnes, -18C, maintained by 2 refrigeration units, capacity 40kg/h each, level indicator. Used on large ships tankers.

8. SAFETY AND TESTS:
Weekly alarm test, monthly cylinder content check by weight +-5%, annual pipe blowing through with air 2-3 bar, 2 yearly internal inspection manifold. Function test of time delay with nitrogen.

9. ALTERNATIVE GASES LINK:
FM-200 HFC227ea design 8.5%, discharge 10 sec, NOAEL 9%, LOAEL 10.5%. Novec FK-5-1-12 5.6%, 10 sec. Inergen IG-541 45% concentration 60 sec discharge. Must have ODNE? ODP zero.

10. PSC DEFICIENCIES:
Most common detention: CO2 room door locked but key not nearby, low pressure tank high pressure alarm not working, cylinder low weight >10% loss, release cabinet not labeled, no operating instructions in language of crew, time delay <20 sec.
---

SOURCE: CO2-System-ULTRA-DEEP-FSS-Ch3-2024.txt
URL: IMO FSS Code Chapter 3 CO2 System
11. EXAM QUESTIONS READY:
Q1 How much CO2 for 4700m3 ER? Ans 3357kg 75 cylinders.
Q2 Discharge time? Ans 85% in 2 min machinery.
Q3 Why two controls? Ans Safety to prevent accidental release causing suffocation - 8% CO2 fatal, 15% causes unconscious in 1 min.
Q4 Low pressure temp? -20C.

12. REFERENCES: FSS Code Ch3, MSC.1/Circ.1318 guidelines for fixed gas, MSC.1/Circ.1312 for CO2 testing, ISO 6183 for design.

2. QUANTITY CALCULATION - MOST IMPORTANT FOR EXAM:
Formula: V_co2 = 0.56 m3 per kg CO2 free gas at STP
Machinery Space: Largest gross volume including casing. Example Calculation:
Engine Room LxBxH 30x15x10 = 4500 m3, plus casing 200 m3 = 4700 m3
For 35% concentration, formula Q = 0.35 x V / 0.56 = 0.625 x V
Actually FSS requires 40% for machinery without casing? Check: For cargo ships machinery 40% of gross excluding shaft but including casing? Simplified: CO2 required = 35% net? Standard used 40% rule: 1kg per 0.56m3 so 40% means 0.40*4700=1880m3 free gas => 1880/0.56=3357kg CO2.
Each cylinder 45kg => 75 cylinders. If 67.5kg cylinder => 50 cylinders.
Cargo hold example: Hold 5000m3 net, 30% => 1500m3 free => 2678kg => 60 cylinders of 45kg.
