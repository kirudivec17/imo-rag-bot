THE MARITIME SAFETY COMMITTEE, 
 
RECALLING Article 28(b) of the Convention on the International Maritime Organization 
concerning the functions of the Committee,

Life at Sea (SOLAS), 1974 (her einafter referred to as “the  Convention”), concerning the 
amendment procedure applicable to the Annex to the Convention, other than  to the provisions of 
chapter I thereof, 
 
HAVING CONSIDERED, at its eighty-sixth session, amendm ents to the Convention, 
proposed and circulated in accordance with article VIII(b)(i) thereof,
